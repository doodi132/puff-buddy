How this works + privacy

Everything is stored locally in this browser, not uploaded. Keep using the same browser to preserve history. Clearing website data can erase it. This page cannot block your vape or send background notifications. The open clock uses saved timestamps and resumes when you reopen the app. The starting goal of 40 is a placeholder, not a recommended allowance. For your first day, log your real usage and set a realistic lower target afterward.
